Our Story

Built on Craftsmanship. Driven by Precision.

Vuka Construction was established in 2018 with a straightforward purpose: to deliver concrete surface work and construction finishing services that actually meet the standards described in a quote. Too many projects across South Africa are handed over with substandard finishes, insufficient preparation, or machinery that was hired at the last minute and operated by someone who has never used it before. Vuka was founded to be the alternative.

Being owner-operated is not a marketing slogan at Vuka — it is the operating model. Horacio Jose Nhatumbo is present on every project from first site visit to final sign-off. He sets the laser datums. He makes the call on when the concrete is ready for the first float pass. He is the person whose phone rings if something is not right after handover. This direct accountability drives a standard of work that is difficult to replicate through subcontractor chains.

Over the years, Vuka Construction has built its own equipment fleet — ride-on and walk-behind power trowels, diamond grinding systems, plate compactors, laser levelling equipment, and commercial pressure washing units. Owning the machinery means we are never at the mercy of hire company availability, we maintain the equipment to our own standards, and our operators work with the same machines every day. The result is consistent output, not variable results.

Today, Vuka Construction serves residential, commercial, and industrial clients across Gauteng and surrounding provinces, delivering concrete power floating, polished concrete, exposed aggregate, slab waterproofing, painting, and paving — all under one roof, all to the same uncompromising standard.

Our Equipment

We Own Our Equipment Fleet

We do not outsource machinery. Every piece of equipment used on a Vuka Construction project is owned, maintained, and operated by our team. This means consistent output, no hire company delays, and operators who know their machines intimately.

Ride-on Power Trowel

915mm pan/blade diameter. Handles 3,000m² and above per day. Used on warehouse floors, industrial slabs, and large commercial pours where consistent speed and pressure are critical to a uniform finish.

Walk-behind Power Trowel

600mm pan/blade diameter. Designed for columns, edges, confined areas, and residential slabs where the ride-on cannot manoeuvre. Equally capable of producing a dense, burnished surface finish.

Diamond Grinding System

3-head planetary grinder with 11 grinding positions, from 40-grit metal bond through to 1,500-grit resin bond. Used for polished concrete, surface levelling, coating removal, and exposed aggregate refinement.

Plate Compactor

90kg vibratory plate compactor for paving sub-base compaction, G5 layer compaction, and bedding layer preparation. Ensures the foundation beneath paving is stable before any surface work begins.

Laser Level System

Rotating laser with dual grade capability for precise slab level establishment on all concrete projects. Eliminates guesswork on falls and ensures slabs meet SANS 10249 flatness tolerances from the first pour.

Pressure Washing Unit

250 bar commercial pressure washing unit for surface preparation before waterproofing and painting, exposed aggregate wash-off after retarder application, and post-project site clean-up.

Company History

How We Got Here

2018

Company Founded

Vuka Construction registered with an initial focus on concrete power floating and paving in Gauteng. From day one, Horacio Jose Nhatumbo operated as owner and primary tradesman — establishing the owner-on-site standard that continues to define the business.

2020

Equipment Investment

Own ride-on power trowel and 3-head diamond grinding system acquired, ending reliance on hired machinery. Owning the equipment gave Vuka complete control over scheduling, maintenance, and operator training — and immediately improved the consistency of output on every project.

2022

Service Expansion

Slab waterproofing and painting services added to the portfolio, broadening Vuka's capability from concrete surface work to full building envelope services. Clients could now engage a single contractor for floors, waterproofing, and external finishes — reducing coordination complexity on multi-trade projects.

2024

Industrial Scale

First 2,000m²+ warehouse floor project completed — a milestone that demonstrated Vuka's capacity to handle large-scale industrial concrete work without subcontracting the core floating and finishing operations. Industrial and commercial clients now form a significant part of the client base alongside residential work.
